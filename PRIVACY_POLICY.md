# Privacy Policy — Minimalist Launcher

_Last updated: 2026-09-24_

Minimalist Launcher ("the app") is a home screen replacement for Android. This policy explains what data the app accesses and what it does with it.

## Summary

**The app does not collect, upload, share or sell any personal data.** Everything it uses stays on your device. The app has no internet permission, no analytics, no advertising and no accounts.

## Data the app accesses

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| List of installed apps | To show your apps in the launcher | Stays on your device |
| App usage (usage access, optional) | To calculate and show your screen time for today | Processed on your device only; never stored off-device or transmitted |
| Daily launch counts per app | To show launch counters and the optional "Do you really need this?" reminder | Stored in the app's private database on your device; cleared daily |
| Your settings (theme, favorites, hidden apps, focus modes) | To remember your preferences | Stored privately on your device; may be included in your own Android device backup |
| Network connection type (optional quick info) | To show whether you are on Wi-Fi or mobile data | Read on demand, never stored |

## Usage access

Usage access is optional. The app asks for it only after explaining why, and you grant it yourself in Android settings. You can revoke it at any time in **Settings › Apps › Special app access › Usage access**. Without it, the launcher works normally but does not show screen time.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your device. You can also clear it at any time from **Settings › Apps › Minimalist Launcher › Storage › Clear data**.
